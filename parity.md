# Parity with the iOS apps

The Android and desktop apps follow the SwiftUI apps, which keep moving. This
file records, for each, the last iOS commit whose behaviour has been carried
over here. `scripts/ios-delta.sh` lists what has landed on iOS since.

| iOS app | repo | ported up to |
|---|---|---|
| Maurice | `maurice` (`app/`) | `2a703c9` |
| Carnet | `carnet` | `5bca94b` |

## How the pointer moves

- During the catch-up (until the first complete version), the port follows
  the state of the iOS apps at the commits above; whatever lands after them
  waits in the delta.
- Each commit in the delta is either ported or marked as having no Android
  counterpart (Liquid Glass, macOS-only settings, App Intents…), then the
  pointer moves past it. Note the skipped ones below with a reason.

## Behind Carnet's full scope, not shipped on iOS either

Carnet iOS is built in one of two scopes (`CarnetApp/CarnetScope.swift`). The
one it ships is `.minimal`: the list of entries at the root, its menu
(Entries, Archived, Notes, Maurice), a book or an article opened from its
entry. Carnet Android follows that scope. What only `.full` shows is not
ported, and is not a lag: it waits for the day the full scope reopens on iOS.

| iOS screens | what |
|---|---|
| `LibraryView`, `ArticlesShelfView` | The Calibre library and the articles as shelves of their own (the books and the articles are in the list of entries). |
| `AppShell` drawer, `GardenView` | The drawer of sections, the garden as a section. |
| `40859643` (Maurice) | Dictation kept on the device by default: Android has no on-device recogniser (no Google service), so dictation is always the household server's. On a hosted household that is the AI provider's, which the server refuses without the member's yes (`403 ai_consent_required`): the consent already holds what the iOS default protects. The iOS setting and its caption describe a device fallback Android lacks. |
| `3ae0075e` (Maurice) | The iOS privacy manifest (App Store). |
| `4d57fcce` (Maurice), `053718f`, `a47d36a` (Carnet) | The Apple icon sets (Liquid Glass `AppIcon.icon`); Android and desktop have their own export (`art/icons-2026-11`). |
| `77c28f2`, `4b041bd` (Carnet) | The iOS build number, and links to the Maurice repo in the Xcode project. |

## Platform differences the parity suite accepts

What `parity/` marks `platform` (11 November 2026): the two apps differ
here on purpose, or because the platform does.

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Subscription | Android asks the tower for Play's catalogue (`/api/app/store/<account>?store=google`); iOS for the App Store's. StoreKit on iOS, Play Billing on `main`, no store at all on `foss`. |
| Dictation (`40859643`, above) | When the server cannot say whether it transcribes (401, 500, 503, an unreadable answer), iOS calls it unknown and lets the device try; Android, with no recogniser of its own, shows no microphone. |
| Account events | The date is the system's own long date and short time in the device's language; the JDK joins them with a comma where iOS writes "at". |
| HTTP | OkHttp repeats a request once by itself on a 408; URLSession does not. |
| Carnet, *Talk with Maurice* | An answer with no conversation id: iOS shows Foundation's decoding sentence, Android "No conversation." |
| Carnet, sharing the card | Android's share sheet takes one text with the card's link last; iOS hands the link as an item of its own. |
| Domain glyphs | Android draws a domain's symbol from a fixed table (`ui/DomainGlyphs.kt`: the icons the night and the editor choose from); any other SF Symbol falls back to the closed book. |
